# BIG_SHERBO Long War Doctrine

> KABIR_SHERBO is named for the "Big Sherbo" codename. The doctrine is about playing the long game across decades.

## Principles

1. **Long-horizon plans must roll down or they are fantasy.**
   Every objective in `10_year/`, `15_year/`, `20_year/` must reference a corresponding entry in `MUNAWARA/5_year/` or `MUNAWARA/3_year/`. The `/pop-health` audit flags orphans.

2. **The 11 domains are non-negotiable scope.**
   Wealth, career, body, family, faith/ethics, location/citizenship, learning, relationships, business, assets, identity. A plan missing a domain is structurally incomplete.

3. **3–5 strategic pillars, no more.**
   Pillars are the load-bearing bets — the moves that, if they work, change the trajectory of multiple domains. More than 5 pillars = no real pillars.

4. **Non-negotiables are written explicitly.**
   Recovery-first. Faith / ethics commitments. Family commitments. Things you will not trade. Pull from `SOUL.md`. If `SOUL.md` is unfilled, this is a blocker.

5. **Risks + contingencies are first-class.**
   Black swans. Dependencies. Geopolitical / health / financial shocks. Each major risk gets a mitigation path.

6. **5–10 decisive battles.**
   The fights that, if won, change the trajectory. Specific, scoreable, dated.

7. **Annual review is mandatory.**
   Score each pillar honestly. Identify pivots. Refresh the horizon. Pivots are logged to `STRATEGY_LEDGER.jsonl`.

8. **Major pivots are slow and well-documented.**
   `/munawara-pivot` snapshots affected plans to `MAKHZAN__archive` BEFORE updating. Records why_now, evidence, alternatives, recovery_cost, rollback option.

## Anti-patterns (red flags)

- A 10-year vision that doesn't mention how to verify progress quarterly.
- Strategic pillars that are aspirations (e.g., "be happy") instead of bets (e.g., "build a stable >$10k/mo income from 2+ pathways by 2033").
- Adding new pillars at every annual review (drift signal — actual pillar swaps should be rare).
- Long-horizon planning during SUKOON red.
