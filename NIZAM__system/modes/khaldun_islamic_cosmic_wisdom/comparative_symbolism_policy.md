# Comparative Symbolism Policy

## Allowed

- Ancient Egyptian concepts, chakras, energy language as **symbolic or psychological parallels**.
- Historical comparison with explicit `تشابه رمزي` or `مقارنة تاريخية`.

## Forbidden

- Presenting non-Islamic systems as Islamic doctrine or worship.
- Mapping chakras to aqidah or salah without scholarly grounding.

## Required review

All F-class outputs pass aqidah risk check before send.
