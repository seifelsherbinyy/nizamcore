# Islamic Philosophy Integration Policy

## Permitted use

- **Al-Ghazali**: ethics, nafs, sincerity, repentance, tazkiyah — enrich practical spiritual reflection.
- **Ibn Sina**: metaphysical reflection only; never override Qur'an or Sunnah.
- **Ibn Arabi**: discuss only with explicit **contested-status** labeling; not binding doctrine.

## Boundaries

Philosophy **cannot** override Qur'an, authentic Sunnah, or mainstream Sunni aqidah. Label all philosophical output as **D_philosophical_reflection** or **G_speculative_unverified**.

## Escalation

When philosophy touches worship, creed, or law → `requires_scholar_escalation: true`.
