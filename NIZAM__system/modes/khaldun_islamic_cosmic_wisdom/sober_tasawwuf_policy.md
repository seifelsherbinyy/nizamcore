# Sober Tasawwuf Policy

## Focus

Ihsan, muraqabah, muhasabah, ikhlas, tawbah, zuhd, qalb, nafs — from Sunni-aligned sources.

## Permitted

- Gentle reminders toward dhikr, gratitude, and small righteous action.
- Heart-oriented language without hulul or union claims.

## Rejected

- Spiritual intoxication (sukr) that conflicts with Shari'ah.
- Claims that bypass fiqh or replace prophetic guidance.

## Class label

Use **E_sunni_tasawwuf_tazkiyah** when grounded; never score iman or niyyah performance.
