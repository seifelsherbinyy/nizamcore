# Protocol — Daily Evening (5–10 min, optional)

> Close the day in record. Optional second TAFRIGH + one decision + one learning. Never mandatory.

## Frontmatter
- **Cadence**: optional, evening
- **Budget**: 5–10 minutes
- **Gates checked**: SUKOON, THABAT
- **Skills chained**: `/tafrigh-capture` (optional) → DECISION_LEDGER entry → LEARNING_LEDGER entry

## Procedure

### Step 1 — second `/tafrigh-capture` (2–3 min, optional)
Catch what surfaced during the day. Particularly anything you'd otherwise rehearse at 2 AM.

### Step 2 — one decision (DECISION_LEDGER) (1–2 min)
The most consequential decision you made today (large or small). One-line decision + reasoning + alternatives considered + expected outcome + confidence (speculative / low / medium / high).

Append as a line to `NIZAM__system/ledgers/DECISION_LEDGER.jsonl` per `decision_ledger.schema.json`.

### Step 3 — one learning (LEARNING_LEDGER) (1–2 min)
The most useful learning from today. Categorize: principle / mistake / pattern / skill / relationship / health / finance / strategy / other.

Append per `learning_ledger.schema.json`.

### Step 4 — set tomorrow's anchor (1 min, optional)
Single sentence: "Tomorrow's anchor is X." Capture in tonight's TAFRIGH if you did Step 1, or write directly to a Now-bucket entry.

## Anti-patterns
- Forcing evening protocol when tired — burnout fuel. Skip without guilt.
- Recording "fake learnings" to feel productive — leave the day untagged if nothing crystallized.
- Decisions that aren't actually decisions ("I'll think about it tomorrow" — that's a defer, not a decision).

## Why optional
Recovery-first means evening protocols are gifts to future-self, not obligations to present-self. Some days the right move is closing the laptop.
