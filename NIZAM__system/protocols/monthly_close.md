# Protocol - Monthly Close (~50 min, 1st of month)

> Finance milestone check, four-week body trend, and optional pivot assessment.

## Frontmatter

- **Cadence**: 1st of every month or first workday
- **Budget**: ~50 minutes
- **Gates checked**: HIMAYAH, SUKOON, THABAT
- **Skills chained**: `/mal-exchange-rate-check` -> `/mal-milestone-check` -> BADAN monthly trend -> optional pivot consideration

## Procedure

### Step 1 - Exchange-rate check

Fetch EGP/USD from at least two approved sources and use the median.

### Step 2 - Milestone check

Compute the three-month rolling average. Record rung status, evidence, primary pathway, next action, and review date.

### Step 3 - Monthly finance review

Record income and expenses versus plan, pipeline changes, and next-month emphasis.

### Step 4 - BADAN monthly trend

Aggregate four weekly reviews. Report sleep direction, 30-day weight trend, training/recovery balance, and red-flag follow-up without diagnosis.

### Step 5 - Pivot assessment

If a TARIQ pillar has evidence-based weakness, schedule an explicit `/munawara-pivot` review.

## Output

- Milestone check
- Monthly finance review
- BADAN monthly trend notes
- Optional pivot record
- Required FINANCE_LEDGER appends
