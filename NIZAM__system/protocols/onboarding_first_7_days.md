# Protocol — Onboarding: First 7 Days

> POP installed. Now what? A gentle 7-day on-ramp that builds habit without overload.

## Frontmatter
- **Cadence**: one-time, first 7 days
- **Budget**: 5–15 min/day
- **Goal**: build the daily morning habit FIRST. Everything else stacks later.

## Pre-day 0 — SOUL.md fill (~30 min)
Open `SOUL.md`. Fill these sections manually (Claude must NOT invent identity content):
- Who I am
- Why I am building POP
- Values & non-negotiables
- Strengths (verified, with evidence)
- Vulnerabilities & known failure modes
- Things AI should never do for me

This is the foundation everything else references.

## Day 1 — first capture
- `/sukoon-check` (1 min)
- `/tafrigh-capture` (5 min)
- DON'T triage. Just capture. Get the feel.

## Day 2 — capture + triage
- `/sukoon-check` (1 min)
- `/tafrigh-capture` (5 min)
- `/tafrigh-triage` (5 min). Sort yesterday's + today's into 6 buckets.

## Day 3 — first SHURA
- Daily morning protocol (sukoon → tafrigh → triage).
- Try `/shura-brainstorm "what's the most useful thing POP could help me with this month?"` — get a feel for the tool.

## Day 4 — first NAQD
- Daily morning protocol.
- Identify one plan you're about to make. Run `/naqd-grill "<that plan>"`. Watch how it pushes back. Watch how the emotional-state gate behaves if you're tired.

## Day 5 — body baseline (optional)
- Daily morning protocol.
- Run `/badan-daily-signal` for the first time. Pick the fields that match your data sources (WHOOP, Apple Health, manual).
- Add the medical disclaimer to your awareness: "Advisory only — not diagnosis."

## Day 6 — first weekly recap
- Daily morning protocol.
- Run `/pop-recap`. Read what POP synthesized about your week from ledgers alone. This is where you feel the value of machine-readable continuity.

## Day 7 — first health audit
- Daily morning protocol.
- Run `/pop-health`. See orphans, stale claims, contradictions. Don't fix everything — just notice.

## End of week 1 — decision point
- Are the daily mornings sticking? **Yes** → proceed to week 2 (start adding `/qarar-decide`, `/mal-baseline`, weekly Sunday protocol).
- Are they NOT sticking? **No** → don't add more. Investigate why. Is the protocol too long? Is SUKOON consistently yellow/red? Adjust before stacking.

## Anti-patterns during onboarding
- Filling SOUL.md with what you wish were true (instead of what is). Future-Claude will misread you.
- Trying KABIR_SHERBO 10-yr vision on day 3 — too much architecture, too fast.
- Adding Phase 2 (strategy, finance, body) skills before daily morning is habitual — Phase 1 collapses.
- "I should be journaling more" — that's TAFRIGH being mistaken for YAWMIYAT. TAFRIGH is capture, not reflection. They're different tools.

## What success looks like at day 7
- 5–7 days of `/sukoon-check` logged.
- 4–7 brain dumps in `TAFRIGH/raw/`.
- 1–2 triaged days.
- 1 SHURA session.
- 1 NAQD session.
- Maybe 1 body baseline.
- 1 weekly recap.
- 1 health audit.

If you have that, you've succeeded at onboarding. POP is now habit, not novelty.
