## 1. Objective

**[FACT]** O5 consumes reconciled financial truth from O1, protection state from O2, decision analysis from O3, and allocatable-capital outputs from O4. It does not create a competing balance, liquidity, allocation, or transaction truth store.

**[AGREED]** Financial NIZAM must continuously move the owner toward stronger liquidity, lower fragility, better capital allocation, higher productive financial capacity, growing net worth, and progress toward long-term strategic life goals.

**[AGREED]** Wealth Growth is not synonymous with maximizing nominal investment exposure. The system must distinguish realized net worth, current liquidity, protected reserves, debt/liability burden, productive assets, goal funding, income capacity, and forecast trajectory.

**[FACT]** Forecasted returns, expected appreciation, projected salary growth, potential side income, and modeled future asset values are scenarios until realized evidence enters O1 canonical state. They must not be counted as realized wealth.

**[AGREED]** O5 must support both sides of wealth creation: capital preservation and capital generation. When expense optimization becomes insufficient, the system must surface income-resilience and income-expansion opportunities through O18 rather than attempting to solve all pressure through austerity.

**[FACT]** The conversational layer may explain deterministic/statistical outputs but may not independently compute, estimate, convert, or restate authoritative monetary values or percentages. Money, ratios, returns, debt changes, and net-worth effects must come from governed engines.

## 2. Governing source decisions

### 2.1 Existing canonical-candidate rules

**[FACT]** The Financial NIZAM constitution requires evidence before interpretation, reconciliation before optimization, protected liquidity before discretionary optimization, uncertainty disclosure, one canonical state, and human control of consequential financial actions.

**[FACT]** Net worth, available liquidity, due balances, future obligations, and forecast cash must remain distinct.

**[FACT]** Existing budgeting doctrine treats budget as allocation rather than punishment. Actual allocations use money already available; forecast allocations remain forecast-only.

**[FACT]** The forecasting contract requires versioned assumptions, scenario, horizon, source references, uncertainty representation, calculation version, generated time, and confidence.

**[FACT]** Forecast-vs-actual error is persisted by horizon and item type. High error or stale evidence must reduce confidence rather than manufacture precision.

**[FACT]** The Decision Engine requires liquidity impact, deterministic effective-cost results, reversibility, timing, goal alignment, option value, uncertainty, and explicit owner constraints.

**[FACT]** The monthly-close contract records reconciled income/spending, debt and liquidity movement, fees/interest, forecast error, unusual events, rule changes, and next-period allocation proposals in a versioned immutable close.

### 2.2 New owner directions incorporated into this overhaul

**[AGREED]** Financial NIZAM is a Personal CFO and Wealth Operating System.

**[AGREED]** Balanced optimization is the long-run philosophy. Tactical posture can move through Survival, Stabilization, Growth, and Expansion depending on financial conditions.

**[AGREED]** Long-term strategy must remain realistic: the system should pursue marginal improvement across days, weeks, and months rather than assume abrupt unsustainable lifestyle cuts.

**[AGREED]** Wealth recommendations must be evidence-rich, tactical and strategic, and must explain how a present decision changes short-term resilience and long-term trajectory.

**[AGREED]** External economic intelligence, market conditions, FX conditions, inflation risk, regional/global events, and weak-signal sources may later alter risk assumptions or scenarios through governed O16/O17 interfaces but cannot directly rewrite verified personal financial facts.

**[AGREED]** The system should continuously compare prior predictions with actual outcomes so confidence is earned rather than decorative.

**[AGREED]** When a user override later outperforms the system, O5 must accept the evidence and update its strategic assumptions rather than preserve the old recommendation to protect agent ego.

## 5. Wealth-state model

### 5.1 Realized net worth

**[FACT]** Realized net worth must come from governed deterministic balance and liability engines over reconciled evidence. O5 only references that result.

**[FACT]** O5 must never add expected income, forecast appreciation, or speculative values to realized net worth unless a governed valuation engine and evidence policy explicitly classify the value as current and realizable.

### 5.2 Liquid resilience

**[FACT]** Liquid resilience is not the same as net worth. A financially valuable but illiquid asset cannot satisfy immediate obligations merely because it improves a net-worth metric.

**[AGREED]** Wealth-quality reporting should therefore show liquidity and protected reserve separately from total wealth state.

### 5.3 Liability burden

**[FACT]** Liabilities must preserve type, contractual status, due timing, effective-cost reference, secured/unsecured status where evidenced, and repayment requirements.

**[AGREED]** Debt reduction may improve wealth trajectory, but aggressive payoff cannot silently breach O2/O15 protection requirements.

### 5.4 Productive capital

**[PROPOSED]** Productive capital is capital expected to improve future financial capacity through a governed mechanism, such as income-producing assets, approved investments, professional capability, or other strategic deployments. It must be distinguished from ordinary consumption and from speculative claims.

### 5.5 Strategic reserves

**[AGREED]** Emergency reserves and protected liquidity contribute to wealth quality through resilience and option value even when they do not maximize nominal return.

### 5.6 Goal-designated capital

**[FACT]** Capital assigned to an active long-term goal remains distinguishable from unassigned wealth. Reassignment requires explicit policy and audit history.

## 7. Financial-regime interface

### 7.1 Survival

**[AGREED]** Wealth Growth under Survival primarily means preventing further deterioration, preserving liquidity, avoiding avoidable fees/default, containing liability growth, and protecting future optionality.

**[FACT]** Survival does not imply that long-term goals are deleted. It means their immediate funding priority may be reduced under explicit policy.

### 7.2 Stabilization

**[AGREED]** Stabilization focuses on rebuilding protected reserves, reducing structural leakage, improving liability quality, and creating repeatable positive capacity for future capital deployment.

### 7.3 Growth

**[AGREED]** Growth permits increasing productive deployment toward long-term goals, investment capacity, capability building, and other approved wealth-building uses while maintaining resilience.

### 7.4 Expansion

**[AGREED]** Expansion permits broader strategic deployment and higher-upside opportunities when resilience, diversification, and downside tolerance remain within governed policy.

### 7.5 Regime changes

**[FACT]** O5 must consume the authoritative O7 regime decision when implemented. It may not relabel the user's regime merely to justify an allocation recommendation.

## 16. Income-resilience interface

**[AGREED]** When financial pressure cannot reasonably be solved through spending optimization, O5 should request analysis from O18 Income Resilience & Expansion.

- persistent negative or weak trajectory despite realistic spending control;
- excessive dependence on one income source;
- recurring erosion of purchasing power;
- insufficient capacity to fund critical strategic goals;
- repeated use of credit to preserve ordinary living standards;
- materially improving expense efficiency but no corresponding resilience improvement.

**[FACT]** O5 does not invent income opportunities by itself.

## 32. Wealth-learning loop

**[PROPOSED]** The learning loop is:

2. record owner decision;

## 37. Failure behavior

### 37.1 Stale canonical state

**[FACT]** Do not issue a high-confidence strategic recommendation. Return limited state and request refresh/reconciliation.

### 37.2 Missing liability evidence

**[FACT]** Do not present a complete net-worth or debt strategy when material liabilities are unresolved.

### 37.3 Missing valuation evidence

**[FACT]** Preserve the asset as unknown/partially valued rather than inventing current value.

### 37.4 Forecast engine unavailable

**[FACT]** Fall back to verified actual state and qualitative constraints; do not fabricate projected growth.

### 37.5 External-intelligence outage

**[FACT]** Continue with personal verified evidence and mark macro-risk layer unavailable.

### 37.6 Contradictory market evidence

**[FACT]** Preserve disagreement and scenario sensitivity rather than average incompatible claims.

### 37.7 Goal conflict

**[AGREED]** Surface tradeoffs and owner priorities; do not silently rewrite goal priority.

### 37.8 Owner override

**[AGREED]** Continue support after override, preserve accepted risks, and evaluate later evidence.

### 37.9 Model underperformance

**[AGREED]** Reduce confidence and review assumptions when repeated forecast error is evidenced.

### 37.10 Persistence failure

**[FACT]** Do not claim durable recording until the required destination is read back and verified.
