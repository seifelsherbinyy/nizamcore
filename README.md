## Why this exists

To reduce mental clutter, support clarity, stress-test ideas, capture learning, plan across horizons (week → quarter → year → decade), and preserve continuity through machine-readable files. **Recovery-first** is the top principle: when sleep/energy/stress signals are red, planning and output downshift.

## Architecture in one sentence

Each top-level folder uses dual naming `ARABIC__technical_function`. `NIZAM__system/` holds the registries, schemas, personas, skills, policies, ledgers, templates, and docs. Three cognitive modules — TAFRIGH (declutter), SHURA (co-think), NAQD (red-team) — and one recovery module SUKOON make up the Phase 1 MVP. Five strategic branches (KABIR_SHERBO, MUNAWARA, MAL, BADAN, AHEL) are designed and scaffolded in later phases.

Weekly (Sunday, ~30 min):
- `/pop-recap` and `/pop-health`.
