# TARIQ — Long-Horizon Strategy

Arabic: طارق — "knocker / morning star." Named for Tariq ibn Ziyad, the long-horizon campaign commander.

## Purpose
10 / 15 / 20-year Long War Map. Covers 11 domains (wealth, career, body, family, faith, location, learning, relationships, business, assets, identity). Identity-level objectives. Strategic pillars + non-negotiables + decisive battles.

## Skills
- `/tariq-vision <horizon>` — craft or update a 10/15/20-yr plan.
- `/tariq-annual-review` — annual scoring + pivot identification.
- `/munawara-pivot` — record a major pivot (snapshots prior state to MAKHZAN).

## Subfolders
- `10_year/`, `15_year/`, `20_year/` — horizon plans.
- `identity_strategy/` — identity-level direction docs.
- `major_pivots/` — pivot records.
- `domains/` — per-domain deep dives.
- `reviews/annual/` — annual review scorecards.

## Core principle
Every long-horizon objective MUST roll down to MUNAWARA 5-yr or 3-yr. Orphans flagged by `/pop-health`.
